About me

A librarian who likes making things work

I am a division librarian in Agusan del Norte, Philippines. I give technical assistance to the school libraries and library hubs in the division: organizing collections, cataloging, and coaching library in-charges.

When a task is slow or done by hand, I look for a better way. That led me to build Project LINK, an online union catalog for school libraries, and a catalog card generator that saves hours of typing.

I am also open to remote work as a virtual assistant, where I bring the same care for order, accuracy and clear records.
